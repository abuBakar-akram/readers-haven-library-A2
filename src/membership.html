IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Membership Rules & Guidelines

Please follow these rules to enjoy a smooth library experience.

  • A valid membership card is required to borrow books.
  • Books must be returned within the selected loan period.
  • PKR 20 is charged per book for each late day.
  • Members are responsible for lost or damaged books.
  • Membership must be renewed every year.
